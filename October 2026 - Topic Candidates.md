## Candidate Topics

### 1. Repair or Replace: The $49.6 Billion Competitor on Your Floor
**Angle:** The appliance shopper's biggest holiday competitor isn't another brand — it's the repair option living in their head, now backed by a documented $49.6B consumer-savings case and right-to-repair laws in 13+ states; a rep with no answer to "should I just fix my old one?" is losing to that force, not to LG.
**Why Now:** Black Friday appliance promos put repair-vs-replace math front-and-center for value shoppers; Whirlpool's right-to-repair compliance (parts + diagnostic access) is now a trainable trust story; NIQ ranks durability (86%) and repairability (82%) among the top purchase factors — this is a mainstream selection criterion, not an edge case.
**Research Signal:** September log, Week of Aug 11 — WOW candidate ($49.6B, 33+ bills across 13 states, BNO News "Repair, Not Replace" report; Whirlpool parts-availability expansion); `Acosta Research/DavidHu/Executive Brief - Consumer Tech Trends 2027` (durability 86% / repairability 82%; ~half would consider refurbished with warranty backing).
**Data to Verify:** ⚠️ $49.6B repair-savings figure — confirm primary source (likely US PIRG) before publication.
**Field Team Hook:** Train a repair-vs-replace decision script (unit age × energy cost × repair quote vs. replacement); equip Whirlpool/Maytag reps with the parts-availability and diagnostic-access story as a trust proof; position replacement as the *justified* outcome when the math says so — and concede repair when it doesn't, which builds the credibility that closes the next visit.
**WOW Factor:** Yes — the $49.6B figure reframes the competitive set: the rep's rival is a decision, not a brand.
**Priority:** High *(earmarked for October during September planning)*

### 2. The Activation IS the Demo
**Angle:** The most-shared 2026 activations make the consumer the content and embed the product proof inside a participatory experience — and Q4 is when brands deploy year-end experiential budgets, so October is the pitch window.
**Why Now:** Holiday floor traffic gives activations their maximum audience; year-end budget deployment was flagged in August planning as the client conversation to have now; Acosta's own shopper research says 90% of electronics/appliance shoppers consider seeing a demonstration important before a final purchase decision.
**Research Signal:** September log, Weeks of Aug 3 & 11 — Experiential Marketing & Buzz (MCoBeauty "The Heist" escape-room mechanic; Huda Beauty throne photo-op; Pixel "Portrait Studio" and Pixel vending-machine concepts; "Find the Feature" timed challenge); `Acosta Research/2025 Thought Leadership/Acosta Group Electronics & Appliances Shopper Journey - Retail Assistance - Feb 2025` (90% demo importance; hands-on interaction and honest expert opinion as the top "good demo" markers).
**Data to Verify:** ⚠️ MCoBeauty participation/impressions figures are modeled on a comparable activation — verify or omit specific numbers.
**Field Team Hook:** Pitch one participatory activation per market for the Nov–Dec window: a Pixel Portrait Studio (AI-enhanced portrait shot on-device, delivered to the shopper's phone via QR) or a Whirlpool/KitchenAid "Find the Feature" timed challenge with a printed take-home. The completion artifact — photo, badge, score — is the social unit; the demo is embedded, not forced.
**WOW Factor:** No
**Priority:** High *(earmarked for October during September planning)*

### 3. Justified Consumption: Holiday Promos Are for Trading Up, Not Down
**Angle:** NIQ's Consumer Tech Trends 2027 — circulated inside Acosta on Sept 3 — names the question every 2026 shopper is asking: "Will this still feel worth it six months from now?" Its most actionable finding: shoppers increasingly use promotions to trade *up*, not to spend less — so a Black Friday script built on "X% off the cheap one" answers a question nobody is asking.
**Why Now:** The report's top-seven promotion events account for 35% of annual tech & durables sales value, and most of them land in the next 90 days; 56% of shoppers have been delaying purchases waiting for discounts — they arrive in November decided, funded, and looking for justification, not persuasion.
**Research Signal:** `Acosta Research/DavidHu/Executive Brief - Consumer Tech Trends 2027` + `2026.09.03 Exec Briefing Email` (value +9% vs. units −1.2% Q1 2026; premium outperformance where the benefit is tangible — robot vacuums +37%, ≥256GB phones +29%, 75"+ TVs +13%; 73% say promotions strongly influence decisions). Corroborated by September log premiumization signals (Circana dollars +2.7% / units −0.9%; J.D. Power $910 average appliance price with rising satisfaction).
**Data to Verify:** ✅ NIQ figures sourced from the report on file; re-check any quoted figure against the NIQ original before publication.
**Field Team Hook:** Rewrite event scripts from discount-led to justification-led: demo the premium tier's tangible benefit first, then present the event price as the trade-up bridge — "the model that solves [their stated frustration] is $Y during the event; here's the six-month ownership math." KitchenAid/Whirlpool: lead with durability and cost-per-use; Pixel: lead with the outcome the AI feature delivers, never the feature name.
**WOW Factor:** Yes — seven promotional events carry 35% of the entire year's category sales, and the shoppers using them are trading up, not down. That inverts how most teams think about discount season.
**Priority:** High

### 4. The AI Shortlist: Winning the Shopper Your Chatbot Sent You
**Angle:** AI assistants are now a formal step in the research chain — but Acosta's own study shows AI recommendations rarely flip loyalty (users are likelier to stick with familiar brands than buy the AI's unknown suggestion), which means the in-store demo is where an AI-generated shortlist actually gets broken or confirmed.
**Why Now:** Holiday research season is peaking and 43% of U.S. online shoppers used an AI tool for product research in the past 90 days; a demo that contradicts what the AI told the shopper creates friction, one that confirms-and-extends it builds trust at speed — reps need to know which move they're making.
**Research Signal:** September log, Week of Aug 3 — Discovery Channel Signals (TikTok 63.1% vs. Google 38.1% for discovery; YouTube appears in 16% of AI-generated answers, Reddit in 10%); August log, Week of July 27 (AI assistants as a formal research layer; the four-stage journey ending at tactile confirmation); `Acosta Research/2026 Thought Leadership/2026 ONE-PAGERS/Acosta Group AI Enabled Shopping Insights One-Pager July 2026` (99% awareness, 56% usage; AI-tool users likelier to stick with familiar brands than adopt AI-recommended unknowns).
**Data to Verify:** ⚠️ Confirm the exact brand-loyalty-vs-AI-recommendation figure from the full Acosta AI study (one-pager indicates 57%) before quoting.
**Field Team Hook:** Train the opener "What did your research tell you?" — then confirm the AI's answer and extend it with the one thing it can't produce: the hands-on artifact. For a Pixel shopper, that's the live result in their hands; for appliances, the running cycle. Never argue with the chatbot; out-demo it.
**WOW Factor:** No
**Priority:** Medium

**Why Now:** Holiday gifting is the year's biggest ecosystem entry-point moment; compatibility mismatch is a preventable January return driver; the open-ecosystem preference is a natural fit for the Google Home/Matter story in mixed-brand households.
**Research Signal:** `Acosta Research/DavidHu/Executive Brief - Consumer Tech Trends 2027` (72% already run up to five connected devices; 78% compatibility importance; 42% open vs. 20% single-brand preference); Running Topic List Section D "Smart Home Reset" (held for a trigger — this is the trigger).

**Field Team Hook:** Make "What do you already own?" the standard opener at smart-home and gift fixtures; build a one-page compatibility router by ecosystem; position Google Home/Pixel as the open-ecosystem answer for mixed households (example framing only — no Pixel sales/share data); flag incompatible gift choices *before* purchase as a returns-prevention service.

**Priority:** Medium
