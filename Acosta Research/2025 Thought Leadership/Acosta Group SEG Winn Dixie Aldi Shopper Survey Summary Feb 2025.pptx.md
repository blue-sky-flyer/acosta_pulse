## Slide 1

Shopper research was conducted through Acosta’s proprietary Shopper Community, February 10-13,2023
SEG/Winn-Dixie/Aldi Conversion – Shopper Insights
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community Survey January 2025
2-in-5 Winn-Dixie shoppers would shop at their converted Aldi location.
Only 15% of shoppers would visit a different Winn-Dixie location…
If there are no Winn-Dixie’s in preferred radius, shoppers would most likely go to:
Shoppers have mixed feelings about the conversion: with some excited about Aldi’s affordable prices and others disappointed about losing Winn-Dixie's familiar brands and rewards program.
…and of those, most are willing to travel up to 5 miles to a different Winn-Dixie.
1-in-3 Winn-Dixie shoppers would choose a different retailer nearby.
1-in-4 Winn-Dixie shoppers experienced store closures in the past year, with most aware of a conversion to Aldi.
