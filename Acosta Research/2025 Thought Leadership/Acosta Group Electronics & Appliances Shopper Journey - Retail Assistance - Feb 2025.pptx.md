## Slide 4

Key Study Findings
CHANNEL CHOICES
Shoppers and browsers explore consumer electronics and appliances at multiple channels with a preference for the large-format stores like Mass, Electronics, and Home Improvement, or Online exclusive (Amazon). On store trips, shoppers seek out both live assistance and informational materials, whereas online shoppers favor independent research.
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community, Feb 2025, [N=947]
FROM BROWSING to BUYING
Browsing is a vital step in the electronics and appliances shopping journey, especially for price comparisons. Most shoppers prefer viewing assortments themselves at brick & mortar for pre-purchase research, and desire more displays with unboxed product. Reviews are a go-to research method - nearly half trust reviews the most.
DEMO DYNAMICS
Most shoppers engage with product demonstrations on brick & mortar trips, noting hands-on interaction and usage explanations as crucial. ‘Good demos’ are most associated with knowledgeable staff providing expert and honest opinions, while bad demos are those manned by unknowledgeable individuals.
SUPPORT CENTRAL
Walmart, Amazon, Home Depot, and Target are top choices for shopping electronics and appliances and highly rated for their shopper support in these categories. Shoppers benefit from various support methods at these leading retailers, including informational materials, displays, looping videos, and staff assistance.
THE POWER OF CLUB
Nearly two-thirds of shoppers purchase and browse at Club, with almost half frequently browsing electronics and appliances. Costco stands out, attracting many shoppers who appreciate their interactive displays and live demos, and who choose this retailer specifically for its support methods on these categories.
THE VALUE OF RETAIL STAFF
They place significant trust in staff and product experts, seeking not only instructions but also opinions and validation. Shoppers rely on staff’s expertise and want tailored recommendations to ensure their final choices align with their personal needs.
