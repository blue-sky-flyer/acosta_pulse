## Slide 33

While frozen foods are praised for being budget-friendly and reliable, misconceptions about preservatives and nutrients lost in the freezing process persist.
Up Next On Ice: Brands should tackle these myths head on with clear messaging to reshape perceptions on nutrition and quality, and highlight clean-label, nutrient rich options to build consumer trust and confidence.
Shoppers turn to frozen for affordability, reliability, and ease, while refrigerated foods dominate the “fresh and premium” space, often seen as higher quality and better aligned with health goals.
Up Next On Ice: Elevate frozen by making it aspirational, moving from “quick and cheap” to “smart and satisfying”. through curated assortments, chef inspired recipes and merchandising that positions frozen as an important meal component.
Frozen foods are a cornerstone of modern grocery shopping: anchoring in-store trips, complementing refrigerated items, and delivering on convenience. Many households rely on frozen foods several times a week, often as a supplement to fresh or refrigerated items to create complete, low-effort meals.
Up Next On Ice: Turn frozen from a backup plan into a frontline solution by leveraging bundled displays, meal kits and in-store signage highlighting cross-merchandised meal solutions and inspire shoppers to build quick, balanced meals.
