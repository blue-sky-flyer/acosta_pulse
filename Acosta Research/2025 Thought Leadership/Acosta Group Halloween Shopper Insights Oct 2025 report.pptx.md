## Slide 3

Half of Halloween shoppers turn to digital sources like Pinterest, YouTube, and retailer websites for holiday ideas.
Shoppers use digital tools such as retailer apps and coupon platforms to plan their Halloween shopping, with younger generations leading the charge. Gen Z and Millennials are twice as likely to be influenced by online content when buying Halloween candy, especially via social media posts, reviews, and viral trends.
2025 Halloween Shopping – The Spooky Outcome
Acosta Group Shopper Community survey, Oct 2025     [N=886]
Halloween shoppers are spirited but strategic, blending tradition with spontaneity as they prepare for the season. From full-blown celebrations to simple gestures like handing out candy—or even non-candy treats—households vary widely in their approach.
When it comes to Halloween treats, most shoppers plan to hand out chocolate candy bars and choosing candy-coated chocolates. A third of Halloween shoppers prefer traditional candy over Halloween-themed options, and 2-in-3 are open to trying new brands, showing a mix of nostalgia and novelty. Mass merchandisers are the go-to channel with over half of shoppers buying their Halloween candy from these retailers
Among those who buy seasonal items, nearly half stock up on their favorite fall flavors throughout the season.
Top flavors include apple cinnamon, pumpkin spice, and apple cider indicating a seasonal consumer preference for warm, spiced flavor profiles.
