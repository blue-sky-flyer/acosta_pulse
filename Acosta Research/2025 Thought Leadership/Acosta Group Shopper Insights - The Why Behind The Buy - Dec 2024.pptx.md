## Slide 4

2024 The Why? Behind The BuyTM  Multicultural EditionOverall Key Study Findings
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community, Nov/Dec 2024, [N=2,178]
2024 Channel “Trend”-setting
While traditional channels remain the most frequented, online and small formats experienced the highest trip increases, driven primarily by younger shoppers. Loyalty programs and digital apps are highly effective retailer tools, engaging over 80% of shoppers.
Shopping: Love it or Leave it?
Shoppers enjoy finding new products, deals, and taking a break from home – but inflated prices and budget constraints diminish trip enjoyment the most. They prioritize good prices on everyday items and demand value with quality from both brands and retailers.
The Shopper’s Quest
Pre-trip planning, including list-making and using retailer tools, is essential. During their trip, while many go straight for what they need, browsing along the way is standard. Most visit Fresh departments (even when Fresh foods are not needed).
Why Ethnic Brands Matter
Ethnic heritage brands are popular with around 7-in-10 ethnic and non-ethnic shoppers, most often bought at Traditional Grocery and Mass. Shelf-stable and frozen categories are picked up the most, with taste and performance being the top purchase driver.
Home Feasts vs. Takeout Treats
While over one-third of shoppers enjoy restaurant or takeout foods weekly, nearly everyone prepares meals at home a few times per week. Besides cravings, shoppers often opt for outside food due to lack of time or desire to cook at home, with much ordering being on impulse.

## Slide 5

2024 The Why? Behind The BuyTM  - Multicultural EditionEthnic Shopper Highlights
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community, Nov/Dec 2024, [N=2,178]
Hispanics are more inclined to shop online and were a key driver behind the 2024 surge in visits to pureplay sites.
Hispanic and Asian shoppers tend to shop more frequently at warehouse club and natural/specialty, whereas Black shoppers are more present at smaller formats like dollar and convenience.
Ethnic shoppers, especially Hispanics, are frequent users of digital apps and digital tools for pre-planning trips.
Ethnic shoppers are more likely to enjoy shopping for groceries and other needs.
Hispanic shoppers shop on Retailer’s Online sites and make digital trips more frequently than other groups
Hispanic and Black shoppers are more likely to find pop-up ads interesting and informative.
When beginning an online grocery order, Hispanics are more likely to use a site’s dropdown menu to check product lists corresponding to specific meals and meal occasions
Hispanic and Black shoppers are high impulse buyers when shopping online.
Ethnic shoppers are especially likely to consider the presence of deals as important to their trips
Hispanic and Black shoppers are more likely to use digital checkout formats when their trips are complete.
Hispanics are highly likely to buy cultural brands that they grew up consuming or using and are more likely to buy brands with bilingual packaging.
Ethnic shoppers shop more frequently each week for foods to prepare at home but are also more likely than other groups to buy food prepared outside the home.
