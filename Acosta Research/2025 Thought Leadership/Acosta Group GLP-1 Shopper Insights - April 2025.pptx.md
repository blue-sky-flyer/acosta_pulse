## Slide 3

Summary
Obesity is a growing concern in the U.S. as over 40% of the population is considered obese
A surge in interest in GLP-1 drugs for weight loss presents an opportunity for the CPG industry to respond with offerings to support GLP-1 users’ needs and to combat a negative impact on the food industry
Concerns about side effects, costs, and safety currently hinder widespread interest in GLP-1 drugs
While efforts are underway to alleviate side effects and cost concerns, the GLP-1 market is already expanding to treat more conditions
Side effects and high costs have led to high dropout rates despite 84% overall user satisfaction
Only 20% of current consumers on GLP-1 drugs for weight management expect to be on the drug 2 or more years
Lapsed GLP-1 users were satisfied with its efficacy, but cost and side effects contributed to nearly all stopping within a year
85% of users experiencing side effects buy products to help deal with them or supplement nutrition gaps, especially younger users
Nearly all GLP-1 users make positive dietary changes that contribute significantly to what they buy
GLP-1 users are more interested in products with key nutrients (e.g., protein, gut health, etc.) to support their weight loss
Lapsed GLP-1 users still try to eat healthier after stopping use of the drug, despite not having the drug to curb their appetite
Most GLP-1 users still enjoy going out to eat, but cut back on quantity and indulgent items

## Slide 4

Implications
The high current dropout rate due to side effects and cost concerns may mitigate the negative impact to CPG … for now
Drug manufacturers are working on new formulations to minimize side effects and pill forms to remove the trial barrier of injections
As GLP-1’s are approved for additional conditions, the drop-off rate may decrease as more users must stay on GLP-1’s for their serious concerns beyond just diabetes, such as heart, kidney, and sleep apnea
Manufacturers are responding now to adapt and innovate based on GLP-1 users’ changing food and beverage consumption habits and dietary needs, which benefit the population as a whole
Everyone can benefit from healthier sweets and snacks that satisfy indulgences, including less processed or better ingredients (e.g., Quest protein chips vs. Doritos)
Reformulations will be needed as GLP-1 users are moving away from traditional salty, spicy, and sweet flavors
Consider packaging and messaging to showcase superior fresh ingredients, healthier recipes, portion control
Restaurants can respond to the GLP-1 industry headwinds by offering smaller portion sizes (e.g., lunch portions anytime) and reframe take-home options as ‘healthy splurges’

The surging popularity of GLP-1 drugs is beginning to obscure the health consequences if people stop taking them, physicians warn.
Why it matters: While many patients can shed up to 20% of their body weight using the injectables, the cost of the drugs and side effects like nausea and vomiting lead many to quit. In most of those cases, their weight returns.

Almost half of GLP-1 users with obesity had discontinued their medication one year in, according to research published in JAMA. Other analyses show even higher discontinuation rates.
Driving the news: Concern about the long-term effects of withdrawal is intensifying as more public and private insurers clamp down on coverage of GLP-1s for weight loss.
A 2022 study of 327 participants found a mean weight loss of 17.3% after 68 weeks of injections with semaglutide, the active ingredient in Wegovy.
But within one year of withdrawal, the participants regained two-thirds of their prior weight loss, with corresponding changes in risks for conditions like heart disease and Type 2 diabetes.
Researchers have observed that people's hunger can return voraciously when they drop off the drugs, possibly because the body's left in a GLP-1 deficit that affects brain signaling and feelings of satiety.

Between the lines: Some obesity medicine doctors are worried long-term weight loss will become more difficult for users who cycle on and off GLP-1s over time.
