## Slide 5

2025 Spring Holiday Overview
Ramadan: Sundown on Friday, February 28 to sundown on Saturday, March 29
Islamic holy month of fasting, prayer, and reflection.
Lent: Wednesday, March 5 to Thursday, April 17
Christian period of fasting and penance leading up to Easter.
Purim: Sundown on Thursday, March 13 to nightfall on Friday, March 14
Jewish holiday commemorating the saving of the Jewish people from Haman in ancient Persia
Holi: Friday, March 14
Hindu festival celebrating the arrival of spring and the victory of good over evil.
St. Patrick’s Day: Monday, March 17
Celebrates St. Patrick, the patron saint of Ireland.
Naw Ruz: Thursday, March 20
Bahá'í New Year, marking the end of the Bahá'í month of fasting.
Passover: Sundown on Saturday, April 12 to nightfall on Sunday, April 20
Jewish holiday commemorating the Exodus from Egypt.
Easter: Sunday, April 20
Celebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ in Christianity.

## Slide 6

While many Spring holidays involve feasts, there are several that include fasting such as Lent, Purim, Passover and Ramadan
Source: Interfaith America
Lent: Christians observe a period of fasting and self-denial for 40 days leading up to Easter
Purim: The day before Purim, known as the Fast of Esther, involves fasting from dawn to nightfall
Passover: The Fast of the Firstborn is observed on the eve of Passover by firstborn males
Ramadan: Muslims fast from dawn to sunset for the entire month of Ramadan
Naw Ruz: For Bahá'ís, Naw Ruz marks the end of a 19-day fast
[Speaker notes] https://www.interfaithamerica.org/article/spring-religious-holidays/

## Slide 7

Many Spring Holidays are celebrations, while Lent, Passover and Ramadan are considered holiday observations of remembrance
Source: PYN HQ, LA Times
Observance
Definition: Focused on solemn or reflective activities, often involving rituals, traditions, and practices that hold religious or cultural significance.
Activities: Observances may include fasting, prayer, meditation, and other acts of devotion or remembrance.
Examples: Lent, Passover, Ramadan.
Celebration
Definition: A way to mark a special occasion with happiness and enjoyment that typically involves joyful activities, festivities, and social gatherings.
Activities: Celebrations can include feasts, parties, parades, music, dancing, and other forms of entertainment.
Examples: St. Patrick’s Day, Easter, Purim, Holi, Naw Ruz.
Overall, holiday celebrations are more social and joyful, while observances are more reflective, typically involving specific religious practices.
A Holi celebration.
