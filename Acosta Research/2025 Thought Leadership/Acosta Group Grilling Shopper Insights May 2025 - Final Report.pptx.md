## Slide 3

For their grilling needs, 4-in-five grillers shop at traditional grocers, while 3-in-5 turn to mass merchandisers. Walmart is the preferred retailer, followed by Aldi, Kroger, Costco, and Target.
Most grillers use seasonings or sauce when preparing foods, 2-in-3 use BBQ sauce, and many are open to trying new foods like seafood, fruits and different cuts of meat to add some variety.
One-in-three grillers only buy items when they’re on sale, and 88% have noticed higher prices on proteins like steak, chicken or fish—making affordability a key barrier.
Two-thirds of shopping trips include foil and soda, while 3-in-5 include paper goods, snacks, and side dishes—showing that grilling occasions extend well beyond the main course, including non-food.
While hamburgers (86%), hot dogs (83%), steak (82%), and chicken (82%) remain the most common choices, many grillers are branching out to ribs (59%), vegetables (57%), pork (51%), and kabobs (50%).
Grilling Shopper InsightsSummary of Findings
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community May 2025
Promos Are Key Purchase Drivers
When grilling items are on promotion, two-thirds of shoppers buy only what they need for their next occasion, yet 9-in-10 “grilling enthusiasts” will stock up —clear, well-placed promotions, both online and in-store, are a powerful purchase lever.
Digital Inspiration Drives Discovery
Half of grillers turn to the internet or friends and family for new grilling ideas, while enthusiasts are more likely to consult cooking blogs or shows. Digital content can be key to what ends up on the grill.
🔥 Heads Up
Grilling Knows No Calendar
With households grilling all year, the opportunities to connect with shoppers are endless: expanding seasonal messaging, introducing new recipes, and enhancing digital content on grilling trends can continue to inspire grillers’ experiences.

## Slide 15

Nearly all grillers are active in Summer, and 1-in-3 will even grill over the Winter months. Two-thirds of grillers don’t need a reason to grill, including 4-in-5 enthusiasts.
Over 4-in-5 grillers shop at traditional grocers for their grilling needs, while 3-in-5 go to a mass merchandiser. Top retailers for grilling needs include Walmart, Aldi, Kroger, Costco, and Target.
