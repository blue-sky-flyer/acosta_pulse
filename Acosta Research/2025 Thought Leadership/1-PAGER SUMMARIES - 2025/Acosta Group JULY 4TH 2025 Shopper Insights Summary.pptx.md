59% shoppers plan to celebrate the 4th of July this year.

Channels Shopped for 4th of July Meal

With July 4th on the horizon, shoppers are actively preparing for their social gatherings with a strong focus on value, presenting a clear opportunity for retailers to support these celebrations with timely promotions and simplified meal solutions.
This year’s July 4th shopper is budget-conscious, brand flexible and planning ahead – many are buying across categories and looking for deals in-store.  Retailers who meet them with convenience, inspiration and savings will win the basket.
