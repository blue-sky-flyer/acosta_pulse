## Slide 1

The checkout experience is evolving. While self-checkout and traditional lanes remain equally preferred, many shoppers - especially younger ones - are embracing self-checkout as essential. Even those who prefer staffed lanes would use self-checkout if it were the only option. Looking to the future, checkout technology is advancing to make in-store experiences even more seamless. Emerging options like scan & pay, app-based checkout, scanner-less systems, and smart carts are gaining momentum with some retailers already ahead of the curve.
