## Page 1

SEAMLESS SHOPPER JOURNEY
Consumers shopping habits increasingly blend the digital and physical shopping 
experiences. Pre-trip planning, such as list-making and using retailer tools, is crucial. 
During a shopper’s trip, many head directly to what they need, but browsing along the 
way is common, with digital apps in-hand to assist during a trip in-store.
DECEMBER 2024
67% of all shoppers use digital 
tools to plan shopping trips. 
94% of shoppers perform at least one 
pre-trip action. 
HALF of US shoppers 
are buying groceries 
online at least 
occasionally. 
“My go to is Amazon. I look on there to see their prices first and then I go to Google 
and do a price comparison... if it can be purchased locally then I have no problem
going out to get it.”
- Ashley (F), Millennial
71% of millennials are buying groceries online at least 
occasionally. 
80% of shoppers have a retailer’s digital app on their phone
Over half of shoppers with a retailer’s digital app use it during most or every in-store
trip, looking for coupons, special offers, and to check product availability.
