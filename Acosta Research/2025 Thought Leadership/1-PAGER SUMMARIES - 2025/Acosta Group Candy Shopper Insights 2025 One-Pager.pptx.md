## Slide 1

Candy shoppers are searching far and wide (and often) for their sweet treats. They are open to new and innovative products, often buying impulsively in store (though online matters), sparked by cravings and eye-catching packing. However, brand trust and experience with the brand goes a long way and plays an essential role in choosing whether to try out a new candy product or make a repeat purchase.
