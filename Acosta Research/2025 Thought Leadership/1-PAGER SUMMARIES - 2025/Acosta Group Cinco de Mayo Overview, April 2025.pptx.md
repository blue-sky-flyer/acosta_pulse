## Slide 1

As Cinco de Mayo approaches, shoppers are well into planning their celebrations, most striving for a ‘one-stop’ shop. Retailers can cater to this by focusing on convenience, in-store displays, and promotions. Leading up to a shopping trip, shoppers are relying on friends, family, and social media as resources to quickly gather recipes and grilling tips to create traditional Mexican meals using authentic brands to bring their celebrations to life; a process which retailers can leverage.
