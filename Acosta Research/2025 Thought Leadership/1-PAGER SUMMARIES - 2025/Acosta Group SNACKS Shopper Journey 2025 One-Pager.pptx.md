## Slide 1

Snack shoppers are steady, but versatile in their shopping habits, picking up their favorite bites anywhere from a few times a week to once a month and are most often enjoyed as between-meal bites. While snacks are often part of a planned routine, they’re also likely to be tossed into the cart on a whim - driven by cravings or eye-catching packaging in store though a good portion of snack purchases are also happening digitally.
