## Slide 3

Football is a major part of Thanksgiving celebrations, with half of celebrants planning to watch and men leading the charge for multiple games. Fans keep gatherings small and menus classic: chips, dip, and wings dominate for food while soda, beer, and water top the drinks list.
Thanksgiving Weekend: Simple Meals, Big Traditions
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community Survey, Nov 2025     [N=1599]
Most shoppers keep Thanksgiving simple and family-focused rather than going all out. Home cooking dominates, yet frozen sides, pre-made biscuits, and packaged desserts help save time.
Nearly 8 in 10 shoppers hit Black Friday, but two-thirds continue through the weekend and Cyber Monday. Clothing leads purchases, while tech essentials like laptops and TVs drive electronics sales.
Most celebrations will feature a frozen turkey with traditional side dishes like mashed potatoes, stuffing and gravy, and drinks such as water and soda. Additionally, 4-in-5 hosts plan to serve snacks or appetizers like chips and dip.
Grocery stores remain the go-to channel, and shoppers lean toward value with store brands and simpler entrees while adding a few premium touches. Many start shopping by mid-November or early Thanksgiving week. Paper goods, serving ware, and décor are common add-ons.
