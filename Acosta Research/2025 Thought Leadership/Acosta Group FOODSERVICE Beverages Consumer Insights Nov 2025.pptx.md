## Slide 2

Objectives
The primary objective of this research was to uncover new shopper insights across a wide range of beverage categories, specifically exploring:
What’s Influencing Change - New and trending lifestyles and habits influencing beverage trends.
Perceptions - Discover shoppers’ perceptions of key types of beverages and brands.
Influencers and Motivations  - what are the top reasons for choosing each type of beverage
Barriers to Purchase  - what’s preventing shoppers from buying more of each type of beverage
Channels - Explore channel nuances, particularly foodservice and retail channels.

## Slide 3
