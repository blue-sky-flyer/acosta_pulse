ChatGPT agentic checkout: Pros and cons for FMCG and retail

Pros:
Funnel compression: Research, find, and buy collapse into one step, boosting conversion.
Merchant of record: You keep the transaction, fulfillment, and customer data.
Lower fees: Projected effective fee of 4%-8% (versus 12%-20% on marketplaces).
New reach: Gain access to ChatGPT’s 700 million active users who already use the platform for discovery.
Transparency: Brand/retailer name is clearly shown throughout checkout, unlike on marketplaces.

Cons:
Loss of marketing auto-enrollment: Frictionless opt-ins for loyalty or remarketing are lost.
Data capture gap: Agents mediating all interactions risks lowering customer lifetime value.
Reliance on OpenAI layer: OpenAI positions itself as the decisioning gatekeeper.
Early complexity: Checkout is for single items today; multi-item/multivendor checkout is still evolving.
Strategic risk: Retailers risk sliding into “pure fulfillment” if differentiation is not clear.
