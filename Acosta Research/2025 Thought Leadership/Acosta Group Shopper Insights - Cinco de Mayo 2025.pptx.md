[Speaker notes] Notes in methodology differences between 2024 and 2025: 
Methodology to determine WHO was a freshly prepared shopper differed from 2024 to 2025. In 2024, we asked ‘what type of products have you purchased in the past 6 months?’ including a list of various products with freshly prepared listed as an option, whereas in 2025 we asked customers specifically if they had purchased fresh (see Q10 below).

[Speaker notes] Story: Despite strong levels of familiarity with the holiday, there is still confusion about why the holiday is celebrated. Many state it’s a celebration of heritage and culture, which while true, is only part of the story. While officially a celebration of Mexico’s defeat over the French army in the Battle of Puebla, many confusion the origins of the holiday with Mexico’s Independence Day, Dias de Lost Muertos (The Day of the Dead), or admit that they don’t know why they celebrate. While confusion around the origins of the holiday are greater among non-Hispanics, Hispanic shoppers are not immune, with many younger Hispanic shoppers also unsure of the holiday’s origins.

[Speaker notes] Story: More shoppers than not believe that Cinco de Mayo’s popularity in the United States is increasing, both overall and among Hispanic communities. Some shoppers don’t mind, saying the promotions are another tool to bring positive awareness to the holiday while others worry that it’s risk losing cultural meaning. 
Recommendation: One-way retailers can address cultural concerns among Hispanic shoppers, is making sure that Cinco de Mayo promotions are done from a place of cultural authenticity such as showcasing culturally authentic Mexican brands and promoting the reasons behind the holiday.

KEY TAKE-A-WAYS
- Most shoppers (3 out of every 4) were familiar with the holiday but there is confusion around the holiday's origins.
Many shoppers weren’t sure why the holiday was celebrated, confused it with Mexico’s Independence Day, and/or the Dias de Los Muertos
- The popularity of Cinco de Mayo is growing and shopper's notice; pointing out the commercialization of the holiday.
41% of shoppers believe the popularity of Cinco de Mayo has grown in the United States over the past few years.
1 out of every 3 shoppers say Cinco de Mayo is important to them on a personal level.
- How shoppers celebrate Cinco de Mayo is influenced by the day of the week the celebration falls on.
37% of shoppers celebrate beyond just the day that Cinco de Mayo lands, “depending on when the actual day is. If it’s in the middle of the week, I usually go to weekend events for it.
- Home is at the center of Cinco de Mayo celebrations & foods are what holiday preferences are centered around.
45% of shoppers celebrating planned to celebrate at home while 27% of shoppers planned to celebrate at a friend or family member’s home
More than 2 out of every 5 shoppers are preparing traditional foods, shopping for ingredients and supplies, and or preparing to grill / BBQ.
Over a quarter of shoppers planned to research recipes and 16% planned to order catering from a restaurant / venue.
