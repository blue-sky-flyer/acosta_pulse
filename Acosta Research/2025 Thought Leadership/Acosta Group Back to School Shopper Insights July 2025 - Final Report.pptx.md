## Slide 4

KEY TAKEAWAYS
The back-to-school shopping period remains the best time for promoting school-related categories and products during the school year
Shoppers are spending most (62%) of their yearly school-related budget during this time, with an average spend of $564 per household
90% of shoppers say promotions and deals are important during the back-to-school shopping period; straightforward price reductions and BOGO deals are the most appealing promotions to shoppers
Most shoppers also expect to find BTS promotions through in-store signage (78%)
Having early deals and promotions available to back-to-school households is important to attracting key shoppers.
Over 1 out of every 3 shoppers consider themselves ‘early’ shoppers
Nearly 1 out of every 5 shoppers began their back-to-school shopping before July
The top reason for shopping early, according to shoppers, is take advantage of deals and promotions; avoiding last-minute shopping stress is also a major concern

## Slide 5

KEY TAKEAWAYS
In-person shopping remains the preference for purchasing school supplies; although, online availability is key for school categories like appliances and electronics.
A 61% majority of shopper households' purchases will take place in store by the end of this year’s BTS shopping period
However, less than half of shoppers will shop in store for electronics (46%) or appliances (43%), favoring online shopping or in-store pickup
There is a huge opportunity to push new brands within the snack and lunch space for K-12 households.
Half of school lunches throughout the year will be brought from home, with 2 out of every 3 snacks brought from home
68% say that when it comes to packing lunches or snacks, they are open to trying new brands
Children’s preferences are a key consideration and are likely to influence parent’s openness to try new brands.
Most parents choose their children’s preferences over cost and convenience
When it comes to the school lunches, approximately half of parents are trying to balance nutrition and the ‘cool factor’
Over 1 out of every 4 parents say their child notices when other kids bring ‘trendy’ foods to school
