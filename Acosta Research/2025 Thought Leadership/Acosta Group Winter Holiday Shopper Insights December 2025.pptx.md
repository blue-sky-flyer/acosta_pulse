## Slide 3

Winter Holiday Meals & Gifts Summary – A Festive Forecast
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community Survey December 2025, [N=1426]
A majority of shoppers plan to celebrate a winter holiday, with most celebrating Christmas. Gift exchanges and home decorating dominate holiday plans, while half of shoppers stock up on baking supplies to create festive moments.
Tradition Anchors Winter Holiday Celebrations
Most meal hosts will cook at home with 39% preparing everything themselves. Yet, 29% of hosts will buy premade dishes from a grocery store or add frozen desserts (42%) or frozen appetizers (38%) for easy indulgence.
Convenience Compliments Home Cooking
Peppermint and chocolate mint are the top winter flavor favorites, while salted caramel shines across categories.  Nearly half of seasonal item shoppers stock up on limited flavors while they have the chance.
Seasonal Flavors Spark Indulgence
Holiday shopping is underway with half of shoppers nearly finished by early December. 2-in-10 big-ticket buyers plan to over $1,000, and 60% of them will spend more than last year. Most accessories or beauty gifts will stay under $100.
Gifting Trends Blend Practicality and Splurge
Holiday celebrants on GLP-1 medications are making menu adjustments like smaller portions, more protein and fewer sweets.
Health and Lifestyle Choices Shape Menus
