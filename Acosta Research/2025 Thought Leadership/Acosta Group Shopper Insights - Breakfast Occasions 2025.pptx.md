## Slide 4

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
Breakfast is perceived as the most important meal of the day, and breakfast consumption is on the rise, driven heavily by Gen Z Shoppers.
Most shoppers agree that breakfast is important, but it holds particular importance among Gen Z shoppers.
Despite a steady to slight decline in food consumption overall, more shoppers have increased breakfast consumption than those who have reduced consumption, driven heavily by Gen Z shoppers who report eating more breakfast in the past 6 months.
Breakfast consumption is driven first and foremost by whether shoppers think breakfast items taste good.
Following taste, other key reasons for the type of breakfast eaten include habits, health, and social connection.
Gen Z in particularly find breakfast fun to make or prepare and see it as an opportunity to socialize with others.
Most shoppers eat homemade breakfasts, though Gen Z also stands out for foodservice breakfast occasions.
This type of breakfast choice is driven by habit/routine, time constraint, health goals, budgeting, and family needs.
Gen Z is more likely than any other generation to utilize takeout, quick service, and sit-down restaurants for breakfast.
While traditional grocery and mass merch remain top breakfast item purchase destinations, 1 out of every 4 shoppers are utilizing quick service, fast casual, and coffee shops or cafes for breakfast - again, driven by Gen Z.
Shoppers are interested in trying new breakfast products driven by (you guessed it) Gen Z, and awareness of functional food categories suggests opportunity among younger consumers (e.g., gut health, CBD, adaptogen infused).
[Speaker notes] Story:

Breakfast is Important
So far, we’re seeing that Breakfast is important to the general population, and this remains particularly true among Gen Z. Over the past 6 months breakfast consumption, though already high is trending upward from the 6 months previous driven by Gen Z. 

Top products for breakfast include staples such as eggs, bread, meat and caffeine though purchases in specialty products show strong if niche interests. The drive behind products purchases is taste - even all other things considered, shoppers won’t purchase if it doesn’t taste good though factors like habits, and social connection all play a strong role. 

The majority of shoppers are making meals at home or heating up ready made meals whereas food service is less popular. However, Gen Z is fueling foodservice in the morning with 39% utilizing takeout or quick service and 36% utilizing sit-down breakfast services.

Reasons that Influence Where Shoppers Purchase Breakfast
It’s a given that shoppers are looking for retail and food service to have great tasting and affordable items while wanting those items to be convenient.
