## Slide 2

Objectives and Methodology (Qualitative Online Discussion)
Objectives
To understand …
Current GLP-1 users’ experience with the drug and the why’s and emotions underlying their eating habits and grocery shopping habits
Interest in a “GLP-1 support” dedicated shelf set in store or online
Changing nutritional needs overall and interest in specific foods targeted for GLP-1 users (e.g., Vital Pursuits, etc.)
Lapsed GLP-1 users’ experience with the drug, how they are feeling now, and the why’s and emotions underlying their eating habits now vs. when they were taking the drug
Methodology
Pre-screened participants for a 2-day qualitative online discussion via 2/2025 GLP-1 quantitative survey
Must be currently on or formerly on GLP-1 in last 2 years
15 participants
Current GLP-1 users:  8
Lapsed GLP-1 users:  7

## Slide 3

Summary
Overall Experience with GLP-1
Both current and lapsed GLP-1 users love the weight loss effectiveness and how it makes them feel, while side effects are a key negative
GLP-1’s can be helpful in reducing "food noise“: the constant preoccupation with food.
GLP-1 users would recommend their friends consider the a GLP-1 to get weight loss results, but with caveats around side effects. Current users generally believe that the side effects of GLP-1 medications are worth dealing with for the weight loss results.
Lapsed GLP-1 users also noted costs and insurance issues as barriers to starting and staying on the drug
Lapsed GLP-1 users had mixed feelings after they stopped taking the drug – overall, noticing increased appetite but no more side effects
Lapsed users say that their experience with the drug would be better if side effects were eliminated or reduced and if GLP-1’s were more affordable
Impact on Eating Habits
Current GLP-1 users report overall reduction in appetite with less desire for unhealthy foods and even alcohol
Eating habits have shifted to healthier foods, but the drug mainly causes them to eat less overall
Weight loss motivates users’ total wellness, particularly exercise, but also has improved their mental health and confidence
Lapsed GLP-1 users try to maintain healthy eating habits once off the drug - noticing that they can get by on less food, but some admit to wanting junk food
Source:  Acosta Group GLP-1 Qualitative Study - March 2025

## Slide 4

Summary - continued
Impact on Shopping
Most users say that they are shopping as often, but it’s the amount of food and the type of food that has changed since they started on a GLP-1
GLP-1 users prioritize reading labels for product information when deciding what to buy to support their weight loss goals and nutritional needs
GLP-1 users also see a dedicated set of support items near the pharmacy as a resource to improve their shopping experience and access to key items
An online dedicated GLP-1 support section would be a helpful way to efficiently and leisurely browse for products in private
GLP-1 users are interested in GLP-1 labelled packaged foods, but note that nutritional content is most important
GLP-1 users would like a variety of GLP-1 focused foods, including “approved” sweets, to complement a healthy diet
Current GLP-1 users are open to free online nutrition coaching and offer suggestions on what would be most useful, but lapsed users are less open to coaching as they’re concerned that it might be a waste of time
Impact on Eating Out
Both current and lapsed GLP-1 users still go out to eat at their favorite restaurants, but make more mindful choices than before they were on a GLP-1
Source:  Acosta Group GLP-1 Qualitative Study - March 2025

## Slide 5

Implications
Source:  Acosta Group GLP-1 Qualitative Study - March 2025
Manufacturers are responding now to adapt and innovate based on GLP-1 users’ changing food and beverage consumption habits and dietary needs
Everyone can benefit from healthier sweets and snacks that satisfy indulgences, including less processed or better ingredients (e.g., Quest protein chips vs. Doritos)
Reformulations will be needed as GLP-1 users are moving away from traditional salty, spicy, and sweet flavors
Consider packaging and messaging to showcase superior fresh ingredients, healthier recipes, portion control
Consider further research into offering a health coaching session
Retailers can respond by making it easier for GLP-1 users and former users to maintain healthy habits
Leverage the pharmacy department/pharmacist and/or store dietician services to promote overall health and wellness for those on the medication
Restaurants can respond to the GLP-1 industry headwinds by offering smaller portion sizes (e.g., lunch portions anytime) and reframe take-home options as ‘healthy splurges’
[Speaker notes] Omnichannel shoppers are significantly more valuable than single-channel shoppers
But importantly, shoppers don’t think of themselves as “omnichannel” or even think in terms of channels…The just want to shop!
Shopping is no longer a linear experience where there’s in-store vs online occasions, it’s seamlessly integrated
Messaging must be targeted to attract shoppers in ways they choose to engage
The majority of grocery purchases are digitally influenced
The store is still important:  
56% of omnichannel shoppers prefer to shop in-store for groceries and household items
Retailers continue to invest in upgrades to both their stores and digital offerings to meet shopper demands
Brands and retailers are enabling shopping in the moment across media platforms

Are GLP-1 side effects worth dealing with for the weight loss results?
Current users generally believe that the side effects of GLP-1 medications are worth dealing with for the weight loss results.
Several panelists stated that despite experiencing some side effects like stomach/GI issues, the weight loss results were “life-changing” and “worth it” for them.
Current users found significant benefits from GLP-1 drugs, such as:
Faster weight loss than could be achieved through lifestyle changes alone
Improved blood sugar control
Decreased appetite and cravings for unhealthy foods
One panelist even lost 70 pounds in the first 1.5 years on the medication, feeling much better, suggesting the benefits clearly outweighed any side effects for them.
Overall, there isn’t a clear consensus among lapsed users.
