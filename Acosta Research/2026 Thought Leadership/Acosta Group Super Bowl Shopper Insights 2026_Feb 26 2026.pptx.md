Chips, Dips, and Wings Defined the Game‑Day Menu

Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community Survey February 2026
Most households watched the Super Bowl at home, shaping grocery trips toward game‑day foods and hosting essentials. Shoppers leaned on Grocery and Mass retailers for one‑stop shopping across‑the‑store baskets – while promotions, media, and emerging AI tools helped guide planning and product decisions.
Most shoppers did their Super Bowl shopping 1 to 2 days before the game, and 4‑in‑10 made a special trip specifically for their menu.
Grocery and Mass were the primary destinations, and shoppers browsed the full store to build their spreads.
Shoppers built menus from classic favorites, with chips and dips topping snacks and wings leading main courses.
Nearly 60% added non‑food essentials such as plates, napkins, and serve ware.
