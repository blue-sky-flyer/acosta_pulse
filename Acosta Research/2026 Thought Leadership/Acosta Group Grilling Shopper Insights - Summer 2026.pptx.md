## Slide 3

Traditional grocery stores remain the primary destination for nearly 8-in-10 grillers, although many shoppers supplement their trips across a variety of retail channels.
Nearly half of grillers turn to internet searches (46%) or family and friends (44%) for grilling ideas, highlighting the growing role of digital inspiration.
Most grillers have noticed higher protein prices. In response, shoppers are waiting for promotions, trading into lower-cost options and switching stores or brands to manage costs.
Nearly all grillers (99%) purchase additional grocery items when grilling; frequently adding breads, beverages, snacks, side dishes and non-food grilling essentials to their baskets.
While hamburgers (87%), hot dogs (84%), chicken (81%) and steak (79%) remain grilling staples, grillers are expanding the experience through alternative proteins, flavor exploration and new recipe inspiration.
Key Grilling Shopper Insights
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community June 2026
Value Matters But Flavor Still Wins
Higher prices are driving promotion-seeking behaviors, but taste and flavor remain the top factor when selecting foods to grill. Successful grilling offerings must balance value with flavor.
Grilling Is a Year-Round Mindset
Summer may drive peak grilling activity, but grilling remains a year-round behavior. Extended grilling usage provides brands with opportunities to connect throughout the year with seasonal recipes, occasions and meal inspiration.
🔥 Idea Fire Starters
Digital Discovery Shapes Grilling
Recipe inspiration increasingly comes from internet searches, YouTube, social media and AI-powered tools, particularly among younger generations. Digital content increasingly influences what ends up on the grill.
