Food/Nutrition Segments 
Health Focused – I actively seek out nutritious foods, avoid processed ingredients, and prioritize health in nearly every food choice, regardless of cost 
Health Aware – I try to eat healthy most of the time, but I’m flexible and enjoy occasional indulgences 
Balanced – I don’t actively seek out healthy or unhealthy foods; I eat a mix based on availability, taste, and mood 
Price Conscious – Affordability is most important to me. I often choose cheaper options, even if they’re less nutritious, to get the most food for my money. 
Indulgent Eater – I eat what I like, regardless of ingredients or nutrition labels.
