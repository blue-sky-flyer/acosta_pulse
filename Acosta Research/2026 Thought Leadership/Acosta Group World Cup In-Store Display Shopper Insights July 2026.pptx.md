## Slide 4

Acosta Group Shopper Community survey, 2026 World Cup Display (N=485 Engagers, N=689 Non-Engagers), June/July 2026
2026 World Cup In-Store Display Engagement Key Findings
Event-themed displays do more than attract attention, they drive incremental purchases and bigger baskets.
The World Cup theme influenced purchases for 76% of ‘display engagers’ – 26% who said it was their primary reason for buying off the display, while 37% purchased something they otherwise would not have, and 58% added more to their baskets than planned.
When it comes to event merchandising, location remains one of the strongest drivers of engagement.
World Cup displays were most frequently noticed in center store (66%), store entrances (65%), and endcaps (62%). Shoppers repeatedly credited the displays with high visibility and convenience, also noting they were "hard to miss" and easy to shop.
Event activations help shoppers feel part of the moment and turn excitement into purchase motivation.
Buying off a World Cup display was about more than the product itself for many shoppers. Fan passion, team loyalty, national pride, and the excitement of the tournament all played a role in motivating purchases and enhancing their overall World Cup experience.
World Cup displays drove planned AND unplanned purchases, creating new demand at the point of purchase.
These displays served as both a shopping reminder and an impulse trigger. While 66% purchased display products they knew they already needed, 31% picked up something they did not immediately need.
Game-day staples scored big, as the World Cup created powerful consumption occasions.
Snacks, soft drinks, beer, and other game-day essentials led purchases from World Cup displays, followed by apparel and fan merchandise. Many shoppers specifically linked their purchases to watch parties, gatherings, and match-day celebrations.
Limited-edition packaging was a difference-maker, creating urgency, collectability, and an emotional connection.
World Cup-themed packaging resonated strongly, with 81% purchasing themed products and more than 8-in-10 saying special packaging makes products feel more fun and appealing. Shoppers were especially drawn to the exclusivity and limited-time availability of these items.
