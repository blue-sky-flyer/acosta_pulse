## Slide 4

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
Health is often the top priority for baby & toddler food shoppers, often over other factors like price.
Quality and ingredients make the top of the list for what shoppers are checking for.
Shoppers are looking for the organic / natural ingredients in support of immune, brain, gut, and other health systems.
During the pre-decision process, nearly half of shopper's research product ingredients before deciding.
Pouches are used more often than jars during feedings, though many shoppers use both.
Majority of participants introduce pouches before their baby is 10 months because of convenience and portability.
Jars are most often used at home for spoon feeding and enjoyed for their usability.
While ‘on the shelf’ options are preferred, shoppers view refrigerated items as fresher and often healthier.
Most shopper’s babies or toddlers are snacking at least 2x a day with many snacking at least 4x a day.
Morning and mid-afternoon are the popular times for snacking during occasions such as post-nap, screen time, or play.
Shoppers are often looking for snacks (e.g., crackers, cookies, yogurt) outside of the baby aisle - sometimes due to price.
Source(s): Acosta Group Shopper Community - Baby & Toddler Food, December 2025

## Slide 5

KEY HIGHLIGHTS
Shoppers are frustrated by the in-store experience and say a lack of variety and transparency in store.
Frustrations points include preferred items being out-of-stock, not enough flavor variety, and ingredient confusion.
Shoppers enter the store knowing the brand they are looking for and their minimum health standards.
A third of shoppers are willing to walk away from the retailer if issues are faced, looking online or at another store.
Nearly half of shoppers are willing to walk away from the brand, if they can’t find their brand on the shelf.
Promotions play an important part in the shopper journey, with shoppers looking for discounts.
If shopper’s health standard for baby/toddler food is met, the majority would try a new brand if it was on promotion.
Most shoppers would also stock-up (buy extra at once) if baby or toddler food was on promotion.
Most shoppers are also using at least one coupon platform, with coupons.com being the most cited option.
Source(s): Acosta Group Shopper Community - Baby & Toddler Food, December 2025
