## Slide 3

Summary
While still a major concern, the U.S. adult obesity rate declined in 2025 due to a twofold increase in GLP-1 usage
GLP‑1 growth will continue to disrupt traditional CPG demand, while creating new avenues for health-focused product innovation for brands and retailers
“GLP-1 Friendly” labeled foods are polarizing to consumers who have never taken them
Side effects and high costs are still a major barrier, but a new pill form is bringing costs down
76% of users experiencing side effects buy products to help deal with them or supplement nutrition gaps, especially younger users
Younger GLP-1 users are more likely to buy beauty and personal care products to manage physical changes due to GLP-1 use
Due to lower prices and convenient pill form, the new Wegovy GLP‑1 pill for weight loss has achieved rapid adoption (launched: 1/5/2026)
Most users experience an emotional lift to how they feel about their appearance
More patients taking a GLP-1 only for weight loss feel a positive impact vs. those also taking a GLP-1 for other conditions
Nearly all GLP-1 users experience changes in their relationship with food which drives healthier buying habits
Nearly half of GLP-1 users experience less “food noise” – constant thoughts about food
Half of GLP-1 users are interested in high-protein and/or fiber foods, and NIQ sales data shows rising momentum in foods with high levels
Most GLP-1 users still enjoy going out to eat, but cut back on quantity and indulgent items

## Slide 4

Implications
The GLP-1 user base will expand more due to the new pill form and other innovation designed to mitigate side effects and to treat more chronic conditions
Manufacturers must respond to adapt and innovate based on GLP-1 users’ changing food and beverage consumption habits and dietary needs, which benefit all consumers
Focus on key benefits like protein and fiber as “GLP-1 Friendly” labeling is polarizing to those not on the therapy
Consider packaging and messaging to showcase superior fresh ingredients, healthier recipes, and portion control
Many changes needed to appeal to consumers on GLP-1s also fit with consumers’ growing obsession with health and wellness
All can benefit from healthier sweets and snacks that satisfy indulgences with better ingredients (e.g., protein-fortified Doritos)
Restaurants can respond to the GLP-1 industry headwinds by offering smaller portions, healthy options, and potentially reframing “leftovers” as a way to bring the restaurant experience home
Besides treating side effects, HBC manufacturers have an opportunity to promote their products as also supporting the emotional lift GLP-1 users experience about their appearance

[Speaker notes] https://www.npr.org/sections/shots-health-news/2025/10/28/nx-s1-5587805/glp-1-ozempic-zepbound-gallup-obesity-rate
https://news.gallup.com/poll/696599/obesity-rate-declining.aspx - 10/28/25


After peaking at a record high of 39.9% in 2022, the U.S. adult obesity rate has gradually declined to 37.0% in 2025. This is a statistically meaningful decrease representing an estimated 7.6 million fewer obese adults compared with three years ago. Meanwhile, diagnoses of diabetes — a lifetime disease that can be managed but not cured — have now reached an all-time high of 13.8%. Both metrics are part of the ongoing Gallup National Health and Well-Being Index.
Gallup calculates obesity using the federal standard of having a BMI of 30 or higher.

The percentage of adults who report taking this class of medicine specifically for weight loss has increased to 12.4%, compared with 5.8% in February 2024 when Gallup first measured it. Usage among women (15.2%) continues to outpace men (9.7%), but both groups have more than doubled their use in the past year. These results dovetail with increased awareness of the drugs used for weight loss, which has risen from 80% to 89% nationally in the same period.

Use of Weight Loss Injectables Aligns With Weight Loss Across Age Groups
Amid increased use of GLP-1 injectables for weight loss, obesity rates have been coming down for most age groups since 2022. The biggest reduction in obesity is found among those aged 40 to 49 and those aged 50 to 64, which are also the age groups with the greatest current rates of usage of GLP-1 injectables for weight loss purposes and the greatest increases in use of those injectables since the first quarter of 2024.
