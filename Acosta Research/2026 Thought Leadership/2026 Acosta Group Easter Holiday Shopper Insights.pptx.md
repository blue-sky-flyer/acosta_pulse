## Slide 17

Easter Activity and Participation Summary
Source: Acosta Group Shopper Community Survey April 2026
70% of U.S. households celebrated Easter this year, making it the clear driver of Spring holiday participation. Most Easter celebrations were social, with seven-in ten celebrants gathering with family or friends.
Easter Anchors Spring Holiday Participation
Households with children had higher participation across Spring holiday plans and activities, from baking and Easter basket‑making to egg hunts and decorating. They were also more likely to host and prepare meals at home.
Households with Children Drive Engagement
Special deals and discounts were the most influential retailer draw during Easter shopping, cited by over half of Easter shoppers, while exclusive and limited‑edition products stood out as a clear secondary purchase drivers.
Value Is A Top Purchase Driver, Though Seasonal Novelty Matters
Easter purchasing spanned a wide set of categories, led by edible treats, with households with children more likely to purchase across categories. Candy dominated Easter baskets, while toys were the top non‑candy addition.
Easter Purchasing Is Broad and Candy‑Led
Taste and price were the top drivers of Easter candy purchases, with displays reinforcing those decisions. Only 15% of shoppers noticed Easter candy out‑of‑stocks, which clustered around iconic items like Peeps and Reese’s eggs.
Candy Choices Reflect Balance of Value, Tradition, and Availability
[Speaker notes] With 1-in-20 celebrants using a GLP-1 medication, many are making menu adjustments like smaller portions, more protein, and fewer sweets.
