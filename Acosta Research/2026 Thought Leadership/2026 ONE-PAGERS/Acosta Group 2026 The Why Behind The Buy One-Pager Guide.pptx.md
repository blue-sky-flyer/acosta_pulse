## Slide 1

The Why Behind The BuyTM
Shopping for Groceries and Essentials
April 2026
Value is top‑of‑mind with U.S. shoppers and has shaped nearly everything about how, where, and why they buy. Grocery and Mass are still the go‑to anchors, but shoppers are stretching their dollars by leaning harder into Value channels and the convenience offered by Online. Trip planning has become smarter, with shoppers scanning for deals and trading down when budgets demand it, all while still caring deeply about freshness, quality, and a pleasant in‑store experience. Get the full picture with our The Why Behind The Buy™ report for a deeper look at today’s evolving shopper and flexible, ready-to‑use insights.
For internal BI access to the full report on BI Portal: The Why? Behind The Buy Report
Sourced from Acosta Group’s Proprietary Shopper Community
Channels & Retailers Shopped
A fast primer on the retail landscape. Check out where shoppers are shopping today and how trips shift across Grocery, Mass, Value, Club, and Online – and who is driving those changes.
Shopping Behaviors & Attitudes
A look at the “why” behind every basket move. Step into the shopper mindset for what shapes their decisions. Learn how they plan, budget, use digital tools, and navigate rising costs.
Trip Insights
A practical map of the journey. Follow the trip from planning to checkout, what shoppers do first, how they move through stores and orders, and what derails or motivates along the way.
Two featured “Spotlights” are included for: Store Brands - a quick guide to the private label mindset, and AI Usage on Trips - tech’s growing role in decision‑making.
Food Away‑From‑Home
Helpful context for how foods prepared outside-the-home affect grocery demand. Understand how shoppers both eat and get foods – where they dine/take out/order from, how often, and how menu prices shape choices.
Ask the Ages (Generational Themes)
Explore how each generation shops differently and how lifestage influences shopping behavior with quick snapshots of the priorities shaping Gen Z, Millennials, Gen X, and Boomers.
