## Slide 1

Frozen and refrigerated foods play a central role in today’s grocery shopping experience, anchoring weekly trips and serving as convenient, reliable solutions for fast, low‑effort meals. While frozen wins with shoppers on convenience and value, refrigerated foods signal freshness, quality, and healthfulness. Some shoppers still hold misconceptions around frozen nutrition, creating a powerful opportunity for education and innovation.
