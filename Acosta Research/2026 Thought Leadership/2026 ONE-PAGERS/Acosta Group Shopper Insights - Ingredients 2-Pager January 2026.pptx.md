## Slide 2

In beauty and personal care, product performance outweighs ingredients overall, but more shoppers are routinely checking labels and believe more product safety regulation is needed.  Major retailers have introduced clean label certifications to highlight clean beauty products, but shopper trust has a long way to go. Specifically in skin care, shoppers are split on a preference for clean vs. proven ingredients.
