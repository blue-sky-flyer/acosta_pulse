APRIL 2026
Affordability is not just something shoppers notice at the shelf – it shows up across the entire grocery trip. While most say they generally feel comfortable affording today’s groceries and essentials, higher prices have encouraged a more intentional approach. Shoppers are paying closer attention to where they shop, leaning into value‑focused channels and being more selective with non‑essential buys. “Tools” like deals, bulk-buying, choosing Store Brands, and shopping online are increasingly being used to manage spend and stay in control when navigating grocery decisions.
