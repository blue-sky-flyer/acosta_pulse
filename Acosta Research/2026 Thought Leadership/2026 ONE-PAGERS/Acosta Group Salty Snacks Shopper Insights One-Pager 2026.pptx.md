## Slide 1

Salty snack shoppers are increasingly seeking higher quality, premium products, with natural ingredients and health benefits helping justify higher prices. Increased purchasing is driven by younger shoppers, particularly those active on social media and engaged with trending products, who are willing to pay more and buy the category more frequently. This is especially true among ready-to-eat popped popcorn purchasers. When choosing a salty snack brand or product, natural and health-related benefits play a distinctive role, while familiarity, reputation, packaging, and promotions remain important purchase drivers.
